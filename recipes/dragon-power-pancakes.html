Dragon-Power Pancakes

Spinach pancakes kids beg for

Hidden veggie: spinach

Prep: 10 min Cook: 10 min Serves: 2 kids Difficulty: easy

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 1 handful fresh spinach, blended into the milk
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • pinch of salt
  • 2 candy eyes
  • 1 strawberry, sliced

Steps

  1. Blend the milk and spinach together until smooth and vibrant green.
  2. Whisk in the egg, flour, baking powder, and salt until just combined.
  3. Cook small rounds on a buttered pan until bubbles form, then flip.
  4. Stack three pancakes, biggest on the bottom, to build a "dragon body".

The 3-Step Cuteness Rule

  1. Cut a triangle from a pancake scrap for the dragon's tail.
  2. Add two candy eyes and a strawberry-slice tongue.
  3. Dust with a pinch of green sugar for 'dragon scales'.

Penny's Notes

Hey Sweet Mamas! This one's my ride-or-die breakfast trick. The spinach completely disappears into that batter, and the second I call it a "Dragon", my daughter forgets she ever hated green things. Freezes beautifully — stack with parchment paper between pancakes and reheat in the toaster for a hectic-morning win.
