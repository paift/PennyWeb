Banana Dolphin Fruit Bowl

A 5-minute fruit bowl trick with a banana "dolphin"

Prep: 5 min Cook: 0 min Serves: 1 kid Difficulty: easy

Ingredients

  • 1 banana
  • 2 blueberries
  • 1 cup mixed fruit (melon, grapes, berries) for the "water"

Steps

  1. Cut the banana in half at an angle to make a pointed "nose".
  2. Cut a small triangle notch from the top of the flat end for the tail fin.
  3. Arrange the mixed fruit in a bowl as the "ocean".
  4. Stand the banana pieces nose-up in the fruit like they're leaping out of the water.

The 3-Step Cuteness Rule

  1. Press two blueberries on for eyes before serving.
  2. Add a strawberry-slice splash next to the 'dolphin' for extra motion.
  3. Say 'Look, he's jumping!' — works every single time.

Penny's Notes
